Definition and Positioning of Diffusion Policy

Diffusion Policy belongs to the method category. Its domain includes: AI models and algorithms. Value chain level: intelligence layer. A visual motor policy represented by a conditional denoising diffusion process, enabling multimodal action distributions. Its English name is Diffusion Policy. Its Korean name is Diffusion Policy.

Mathematical and Theoretical Foundations of Diffusion Policy

Diffusion Policy is built upon relevant mathematical theories and physical principles. Understanding its assumptions, constraints, and derivation process is a prerequisite for correctly applying this method. Specifically, attention must be paid to its input space, output space, objective function, and guarantees of convergence or stability. In humanoid robots, which are high-dimensional, underactuated, and strongly coupled systems, Diffusion Policy typically requires a balance between real-time performance, precision, and robustness.

Algorithm Steps and Implementation Key Points

In the practical implementation of Diffusion Policy, it is necessary to clarify initialization conditions, iteration rules, stopping criteria, and parameter tuning strategies. Reasonable selection of numerical methods, linear algebra solvers, and parallel computing strategies can significantly improve computational efficiency and stability. At the same time, engineering constraints such as model errors, sensor noise, and actuator saturation should be fully considered to ensure reliable operation of the algorithm on real platforms.

Typical Applications and Limitations

Diffusion Policy can be applied to various aspects of humanoid robots, including motion planning, control optimization, state estimation, and learning algorithms. However, its computational complexity, dependence on model accuracy, and online adaptation capabilities remain key issues to address in practical deployment.
