## 概述
R-noid 是 Robot.com 进入人形劳动力解决方案市场的首款产品，专为解决餐饮业高达 130% 的人员流动率、仓库拣选员平均仅 1.2 年的任职期以及酒店业 67% 以上的关键岗位空缺等结构性用工难题。该机器人采用无腿设计，配备双 7 自由度手臂、4 自由度铰接躯干（垂直工作范围 0-1.9 米）以及全向移动底座，可在狭窄空间灵活作业。其运行基于 Physical Intelligence 的 π0.7 视觉-语言-动作模型，支持自然语言指令驱动的通用操作，并与 Yukai Engineering 合作开发了名为 R-soul 的表情与行为系统以增强人机信任。Robot.com 将在 Automate 2026 展会上展示 R-noid 及其现有机器人产品线。

## 核心内容
### 产品定位与商业模式
R-noid 是 Robot.com 推出的首款人形劳动力解决方案，采用机器人即服务（RaaS）模式部署。从首次客户现场访问到自主运行，整个流程仅需 8-12 周。该机器人针对餐饮、工业、物流、医疗、酒店及体验服务六大垂直行业，提供五大初始解决方案类别：Restaurant Assistant、Packer、Picker、Folder 和 Host。

### 核心技术架构
- **自主大脑**：与 FieldAI 合作，将其通用 Field Foundation Models (FFMs) 集成至 R-noid，作为自主运行层。FFM 提供三项核心功能：在无预设信息或基础设施的动态真实环境中安全可靠运行；通过物理约束的 AI 模型防止模型幻觉；协调多机器人协同作业。
- **操作模型**：采用 Physical Intelligence 的 π0.7 视觉-语言-动作模型，支持通用操作。该模型可读取自然语言指令，观察场景后生成手臂与手部动作，适应物体、布局和顺序的变化。单一模型即可覆盖包装、拣选和折叠任务，新增任务时无需重新设计机器人。
- **机械本体**：配备双 7 自由度（DoF）手臂、4 自由度铰接躯干（垂直工作范围 0-1.9 米）以及全向移动底座，可在狭窄拥挤空间重新定位。
- **人机交互**：与日本 Yukai Engineering 合作开发 R-soul 表情与行为系统，通过材料、制造和交互设计，使机器人能在数秒内赢得人类信任，传达意图、状态和个性。

### 部署进展与合作伙伴
- **灯塔部署**：R-noid Packer 已在一家获奖高尔夫球场投入运营，负责现场订单包装；Packer 类别正推进至一家大型食品制造工厂的生产线，早期结果验证了其规模化端线能力。Picker 可直接集成至现有物流拣选端口，无需设施改造。
- **合作伙伴**：Formic 担任人形解决方案的部署合作伙伴，协助客户在真实生产环境中试点、部署和扩展自动化。其他支持方包括 NVIDIA Robotics、Astribot、FieldAI、Physical Intelligence、Robots for America 等。
- **展示计划**：Robot.com 将于 2026 年 6 月 22-25 日在芝加哥 Automate 2026 展会的人形展馆（展位 1592）展示 R-noid 及其现有 R-kiwi、R-kiwi+ 和 R-cargo 解决方案。

### 关键数据与结论
- 初始阶段支持 5 大解决方案类别、19 项可部署任务。
- 部署周期：从首次现场访问到自主运行仅需 8-12 周。
- 目标解决的结构性问题：快餐业员工流动率超 130%，仓库拣选员平均任职期仅 1.2 年，67% 以上酒店经营者报告关键岗位空缺。
- CEO Felipe Chavez Cortes 强调：“未来的工作不是更少的人，而是让人们从消耗性工作中解放出来，专注于技艺、关怀和客户。”

Robot.com is working with FieldAI to bring its general-purpose Field Foundation Models (FFMs) to R-noid as the autonomy brain. FFMs serve as an operational AI layer that generalizes across robots and environments and serves three roles: enabling safe and reliable operations in dynamic, real-world spaces without prior information or supporting infrastructure; preventing model hallucinations through physics-grounded AI models; and coordinating multiple robots working together. The body that work runs on is built for reach and stability: dual 7-degree-of-freedom (DoF) arms, a 4-DoF articulated torso with 0 to 1.9m of vertical reach, and a holonomic mobile base that lets R-noid reposition in tight, busy spaces. For the robot's design language and character, Robot.com partnered with Yukai Engineering, a Japanese studio known for emotionally expressive consumer robots. Yukai advised on materials, manufacturing, and interaction design, and the collaboration produced R-soul, the expression and behavior system designed to earn people's trust in seconds. It's a goal Robot.com has pursued since 2017: building robots that open people's hearts and minds to the future of technology. R-soul lets the robot communicate intent, status, and personality. The dexterity comes from Physical Intelligence. R-noid runs on ?0.7, Physical Intelligence's vision-language-action model built for generalist manipulation. It reads a natural-language instruction, looks at the scene in front of it, and produces the arm and hand movements to carry out the task, adapting as objects, layout, and order change. One model spans packing, picking, and folding, so adding a task means extending the same system rather than engineering a new robot for each job. At launch, R-noid can perform 19 deployable tasks across five categories. Lighthouse deployments are already underway, demonstrating the new humanoids' speed-to-impact on business performance. The R-noid Packer is live at an award-winning golf course, handling on-site order packing operations. The Packer category is also moving toward production at a major food manufacturing facility, with early results validating R-noid's end-of-line capabilities at scale. The Picker is designed to integrate directly into existing pick ports across logistics operations, with no facility retrofit required. Formic serves as Robot.com's deployment partner for humanoid solutions, helping customers pilot, deploy, and scale automation in production environments. "Our answer to 'how long will this take?' is weeks, not years," said David Rodriguez, Co-Founder of Robot.com. "With thoughtful hardware design, best-in-class software, and our proven platform, we can have a robot doing real work in your facility within weeks of the first conversation. No other humanoid platform can make that claim." Robot.com's fleet is built on NVIDIA's full robotics stack; the robots run on NVIDIA Jetson modules, which power the robot's perception, planning, and control stack on-device — delivering the low-latency inference real-world operations demand. Across its development cycle, Robot.com uses NVIDIA Isaac Sim to simulate, validate, and stress-test each robot before deployment, ensuring reliability before any unit touches a customer floor. In addition to its Automate debut, R-noid will be among the featured players in Robot.com's first appearance at Cannes Lions, where the company is the official Robotics Innovation Partner for PMG's AI & Tech Sandbox. - **조작 모델**: Physical Intelligence의 π0.7 비전-언어-행동 모델을 채택하여 범용 조작을 지원합니다. 이 모델은 자연어 명령을 읽고, 장면을 관찰한 후 팔과 손의 동작을 생성하며, 객체, 레이아웃, 순서 변화에 적응합니다. 단일 모델로 포장, 피킹, 폴딩 작업을 모두 처리할 수 있으며, 새 작업 추가 시 로봇을 재설계할 필요가 없습니다.
- **기계 본체**: 7자유도(DoF) 양팔, 4자유도 관절형 몸통(수직 작업 범위 0~1.9m), 전방향 이동 베이스를 갖춰 좁고 혼잡한 공간에서도 위치를 재조정할 수 있습니다.
- **인간-로봇 상호작용**: 일본 Yukai Engineering과 협력해 R-soul 표정 및 행동 시스템을 개발했습니다. 재료, 제조, 상호작용 설계를 통해 로봇이 수 초 내에 인간의 신뢰를 얻고, 의도, 상태, 개성을 전달할 수 있게 합니다.

### 배포 진행 상황 및 파트너
- **랜턴 배포**: R-noid Packer는 수상 경력이 있는 골프장에서 운영 중이며, 현장 주문 포장을 담당합니다. Packer 카테고리는 대형 식품 제조 공장의 생산 라인으로 확장 중이며, 초기 결과는 대규모 엔드라인(end-of-line) 역량을 검증했습니다. Picker는 시설 개조 없이 기존 물류 피킹 포트에 직접 통합할 수 있습니다.
- **파트너**: Formic이 휴머노이드 솔루션의 배포 파트너로 활동하며, 고객이 실제 생산 환경에서 자동화를 파일럿, 배포, 확장할 수 있도록 지원합니다. 기타 지원 기관으로는 NVIDIA Robotics, Astribot, FieldAI, Physical Intelligence, Robots for America 등이 있습니다.
- **전시 계획**: Robot.com은 2026년 6월 22~25일 시카고 Automate 2026 전시회의 휴머노이드 전시관(부스 1592)에서 R-noid와 기존 R-kiwi, R-kiwi+, R-cargo 솔루션을 선보일 예정입니다.
