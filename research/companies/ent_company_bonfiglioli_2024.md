## 概述

邦飞利（Bonfiglioli）是一家成立于 1956 年的意大利工业传动与驱动技术供应商，总部位于博洛尼亚，产品涵盖行星减速机、减速电机、变频器及移动机械驱动方案。在人形机器人供应链中，它属于上游零部件制造商（compon「制造商」），与哈默纳科、纳博特斯克、Wittenstein 等共同构成精密减速器的高端竞争格局。它真正改变的不是单一减速器的性能参数，而是工业传动经验向机器人关节高扭矩密度需求迁移的工程路径。

### 为什么存在：痛点与历史定位

精密减速器市场长期存在一个结构性矛盾：**高精度与高扭矩密度难以兼得**。谐波减速器（以哈默纳科为代表）依赖柔轮弹性变形，齿面精度要求 IT5–IT6 级，工艺壁垒极高；RV 减速器（以纳博特斯克为代表）的摆线轮磨削与曲柄轴相位精度同样构成高门槛。这两类产品在工业机器人关节中占据主导，但它们的核心设计目标并非针对人形机器人特有的高动态响应与反驱透明度需求。

邦飞利的历史定位恰恰落在这一空白地带。作为一家拥有超过 60 年行业经验的意大利传动企业，它在精密行星减速器领域积累深厚——行星减速器通过多级齿轮啮合实现减速，天然具备高扭矩密度与模块化优势，且背隙控制虽不及谐波/RV 那般极致，但在重载场景下可靠性更优。邦飞利将这一能力从风电、矿山、物料搬运等传统工业场景迁移至机器人领域，为整机厂提供了谐波/RV 之外的第三种精密传动选择。

### 原理拆解

**① 行星减速器的扭矩放大机制**

行星减速器由太阳轮、行星轮、内齿圈和行星架组成。动力从太阳轮输入，经行星轮分流至内齿圈与行星架，实现减速增扭。其核心优势在于**功率分流**——多个行星轮同时参与啮合，载荷均匀分布，使得同等体积下能传递的扭矩远高于定轴齿轮传动。

输出扭矩与减速比的关系可表示为：

$$
T_{\text{out}} = T_{\text{in}} \cdot i \cdot \eta
$$

其中 \( T_{\text{in}} \) 为输入扭矩，\( i \) 为减速比，\( \eta \) 为传动效率（行星减速器单级效率通常在 95% 以上，工程判断）。高减速比意味着电机可以运行在高效区间，同时输出端获得放大后的扭矩。

**② 300M 系列的模块化设计逻辑**

300M 系列行星减速机是邦飞利在机器人领域的代表产品，其设计哲学是**以模块化覆盖宽谱系需求**。该系列提供 20 种框号尺寸，减速比范围 3.4:1 – 5,000:1，输出扭矩范围 1,000 – 450,000 N·m（系列范围）。这种"细粒度尺寸阶梯 + 宽减速比覆盖"的策略，使得整机厂可以在不更换减速器平台的前提下，通过选配不同框号与减速比来适配不同关节的负载需求——从手腕小扭矩关节到髋部大扭矩关节，共用同一套设计语言与装配工艺。

**③ 从工业传动到机器人关节的迁移路径**

邦飞利在人形机器人领域的价值，不在于发明新的传动原理，而在于将工业传动领域积累的**可靠性验证体系**迁移至机器人场景。风电变桨、矿山输送等应用对减速器的疲劳寿命与极端工况适应性要求极高，这些经验直接转化为机器人关节所需的长期运行可靠性。它真正改变的不是减速器的齿形设计，而是"精密传动如何在高冲击、变负载、长寿命约束下保持一致性"的工程答案。

## Overview

Bonfiglioli is an Italian industrial transmission and drive technology supplier founded in 1956, headquartered in Bologna. Its products cover planetary gearboxes, geared motors, variable frequency drives, and mobile machinery drive solutions. In the humanoid robot supply chain, it belongs to the upstream component manufacturer category, forming the high-end competitive landscape of precision reducers alongside Harmonic Drive, Nabtesco, and Wittenstein. What it truly changes is not the performance parameters of a single reducer, but the engineering pathway through which industrial transmission experience migrates toward the high-torque-density requirements of robot joints.

### Why It Exists: Pain Points and Historical Positioning

The precision reducer market has long faced a structural contradiction: **high precision and high torque density are difficult to achieve simultaneously**. Harmonic reducers (represented by Harmonic Drive) rely on elastic deformation of the flexspline, requiring tooth surface precision at IT5–IT6 levels, creating extremely high process barriers; RV reducers (represented by Nabtesco) also impose high barriers through cycloidal wheel grinding and crankshaft phase accuracy. These two product types dominate industrial robot joints, but their core design goals are not aimed at the high dynamic response and back-drivability transparency requirements unique to humanoid robots.

Bonfiglioli's historical positioning falls precisely into this gap. As an Italian transmission enterprise with over 60 years of industry experience, it has accumulated deep expertise in precision planetary reducers—planetary reducers achieve speed reduction through multi-stage gear meshing, naturally offering high torque density and modular advantages, and while backlash control is not as extreme as harmonic/RV reducers, they offer better reliability in heavy-load scenarios. Bonfiglioli has migrated this capability from traditional industrial scenarios such as wind power, mining, and material handling into the robotics field, providing OEMs with a third precision transmission option beyond harmonic and RV reducers.

### Principle Breakdown

**① Torque amplification mechanism of planetary reducers**

A planetary reducer consists of a sun gear, planet gears, a ring gear, and a planet carrier. Power is input through the sun gear, distributed through the planet gears to the ring gear and planet carrier, achieving speed reduction and torque increase. Its core advantage lies in **power splitting**—multiple planet gears engage simultaneously, distributing the load evenly, allowing it to transmit far more torque at the same volume than fixed-axis gear transmissions.

The relationship between output torque and reduction ratio can be expressed as:

$$
T_{\text{out}} = T_{\text{in}} \cdot i \cdot \eta
$$

where \( T_{\text{in}} \) is the input torque, \( i \) is the reduction ratio, and \( \eta \) is the transmission efficiency (single-stage efficiency of planetary reducers is typically above 95%, based on engineering judgment). A high reduction ratio means the motor can operate in its efficient range while the output end receives amplified torque.

**② Modular design logic of the 300M series**

The 300M series planetary gearbox is Bonfiglioli's representative product in the robotics field, with a design philosophy of **covering a wide spectrum of needs through modularity**. This series offers 20 frame sizes, a reduction ratio range of 3.4:1 – 5,000:1, and an output torque range of 1,000 – 450,000 N·m (series range). This strategy of "fine-grained size steps + wide reduction ratio coverage" allows OEMs to adapt to different joint load requirements by selecting different frame sizes and reduction ratios without changing the reducer platform—from small-torque wrist joints to high-torque hip joints, all sharing the same design language and assembly processes.

**③ Migration path from industrial transmission to robot joints**

Bonfiglioli's value in the humanoid robot field lies not in inventing new transmission principles, but in migrating the **reliability validation system** accumulated in industrial transmission to robotic scenarios. Applications such as wind turbine pitch control and mining conveying impose extremely high requirements on reducer fatigue life and extreme-condition adaptability, and these experiences directly translate into the long-term operational reliability required for robot joints. What it truly changes is not the gear tooth design of reducers, but the engineering answer to "how precision transmission maintains consistency under high impact, variable loads, and long-life constraints."

### Limitations and Boundaries

Bonfiglioli has clear boundaries in the humanoid robot field:

1. **Non-zero backlash**: The multi-stage gear meshing of planetary reducers inevitably introduces backlash, which can be reduced through preloading processes but cannot reach the zero-backlash level of harmonic reducers. For joints requiring extremely high positioning accuracy (such as fingers), Bonfiglioli is not the optimal solution.
2. **Process barriers lower than harmonic/RV**: Harmonic reducers require flexspline tooth profile precision at IT5–IT6 levels, and RV reducers' cycloidal wheel grinding also constitutes a high barrier. Bonfiglioli's planetary reducers have relatively lower process barriers, meaning they are also more substitutable.
3. **No dedicated humanoid robot product line yet**: Bonfiglioli's product system is centered on industrial transmission and has not yet launched a dedicated series for humanoid robot joints (to be confirmed). Its entry into the robotics field is more of a "downward migration of industrial products" than "robot-native design."
4. **Competitive pressure**: In the humanoid robot supply chain, manufacturers such as Harmonic Drive and Leaderdrive have already established strong share barriers, and Bonfiglioli needs to demonstrate the comprehensive cost-performance advantage of its planetary solutions in joint scenarios.

### Common Misconceptions

1. **"Bonfiglioli is a robot-specific reducer manufacturer"**—Incorrect. Its core markets are industrial fields such as wind power, mining, and material handling; robotics is just one application scenario for its precision planetary reducers.
2. **"Planetary reducers are less precise than harmonic reducers, so they are unsuitable for robots"**—Inaccurate. Different joints in humanoid robots have vastly different precision requirements: finger joints need zero backlash, while high-torque hip/knee joints prioritize torque density and reliability. Planetary reducers are entirely viable in the latter scenario.
3. **"The 300M series covering 3.4:1 – 5,000:1 means a single unit is adjustable"**—Incorrect. This is the series range; the reduction ratio of an actual individual reducer is a fixed value achieved through different gear ratios.

### 원리 분석

**① 유성 감속기의 토크 증폭 메커니즘**

유성 감속기는 선 기어, 유성 기어, 링 기어 및 캐리어로 구성됩니다. 동력은 선 기어로 입력되고, 유성 기어를 통해 링 기어와 캐리어로 분기되어 감속 및 토크 증폭을 실현합니다. 핵심 이점은 **동력 분할**에 있습니다. 여러 유성 기어가 동시에 맞물려 하중이 균등하게 분포되므로, 동일 체적에서 전달할 수 있는 토크가 고정축 기어 전동보다 훨씬 높습니다.

출력 토크와 감속비의 관계는 다음과 같이 표현됩니다:

$$
T_{\text{out}} = T_{\text{in}} \cdot i \cdot \eta
$$

여기서 \( T_{\text{in}} \) 은 입력 토크, \( i \) 는 감속비, \( \eta \) 는 전동 효율입니다(유성 감속기의 단단 효율은 일반적으로 95% 이상, 공학적 판단). 높은 감속비는 모터가 고효율 구간에서 작동할 수 있게 하면서, 출력단에서 증폭된 토크를 얻을 수 있게 합니다.

**② 300M 시리즈의 모듈화 설계 논리**

300M 시리즈 유성 감속기는 로봇 분야에서 본피글리오니의 대표 제품으로, 설계 철학은 **모듈화로 광범위한 스펙트럼 수요를 포괄**하는 것입니다. 이 시리즈는 20가지 프레임 크기를 제공하며, 감속비 범위는 3.4:1 – 5,000:1, 출력 토크 범위는 1,000 – 450,000 N·m(시리즈 범위)입니다. 이러한 "세밀한 크기 계단 + 넓은 감속비 커버리지" 전략은 완성차 업체가 감속기 플랫폼을 교체하지 않고도 다양한 프레임 크기와 감속비를 선택하여 서로 다른 관절의 부하 요구에 적응할 수 있게 합니다. 손목의 저토크 관절부터 엉덩이의 고토크 관절까지 동일한 설계 언어와 조립 공정을 공유합니다.

**③ 산업용 동력전달에서 로봇 관절로의 이전 경로**

휴머노이드 로봇 분야에서 본피글리오니의 가치는 새로운 동력전달 원리를 발명하는 것이 아니라, 산업용 동력전달 분야에서 축적된 **신뢰성 검증 체계**를 로봇 시나리오로 이전하는 데 있습니다. 풍력 피치 제어, 광산 컨베이어 등 응용 분야는 감속기의 피로 수명과 극한 조건 적응성에 매우 높은 요구를 하며, 이러한 경험은 로봇 관절에 필요한 장기 운전 신뢰성으로 직접 전환됩니다. 이 회사가 진정으로 바꾸는 것은 감속기의 치형 설계가 아니라, "정밀 동력전달이 고충격, 가변 하중, 장수명 제약 하에서 일관성을 유지하는 방법"에 대한 공학적 답입니다.
